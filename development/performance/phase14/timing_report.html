Method and provenance

Warm measurement

Four independent workers per version, 12 samples per case, synchronized actual returns and balanced case order. Windows worker affinity is 0xffff, covering performance-class logical processors 0–15. Scalar, batch and host workloads use their recorded fixed repetition counts.

Aggregation and spread

Each headline duration is the median of four worker medians. Warm whiskers show the range of those four medians. Cold whiskers show the range of four fresh-process calls. These ranges describe observed spread.

Allowances

Each case uses its saved limit. Most warm cases allow 10% overhead. The declared closed-model PG 1,024-state cohort allows 40%, and state inspection/construction include additive allowances. Setup and cold calls have their own recorded formulas. Eleven warm cases exceed a strict 10% increase, and five exceed their declared budgets.

Returned work and correctness

Current PG full states materialize 22 properties, compared with 13 in the release. Current compiled consumers return their real status arrays. Benchmark comparisons passed, and the full HEOS/REFPROP suite passed 3,029 tests with 54,272 audited property comparisons.
