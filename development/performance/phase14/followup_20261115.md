Previously, both arms of the large-batch conditional separately traced the
complete property equations and independent-coordinate preservation. A private,
module-level jitted `_resolved_properties` helper now stages those equations
once for reuse by both arms. Input-pair and error-policy selectors are static;
coordinates, model parameters and the shared heat capacity remain dynamic.

Property evaluation stays inside the conditional arms. Hoisting complete arrays
outside the conditional could introduce extra branch copies or repeated guard
fusion, so that different transformation is not part of this change. All 22 PG
properties, status precedence, clipping, the 64-element threshold, reference
semantics and input/model differentiation remain intact. No setup proof or
unchecked-state shortcut has been introduced.

## Matched cold measurements

The [first cold comparison](../results/phase14/followup_20261115/helper_cold/summary.json)
uses two fresh workers per version and mode for complete HP batches of 1,024.
Whole first-public-call medians are **41.928 ms PyPI**, **147.401 ms C3** and
**135.788 ms candidate**. The unchanged allowance,
$1.5t_{old}+100\,\mathrm{ms}$, is **162.892 ms** in this comparison.

The matched candidate improvement is approximately **8% versus C3**. The earlier
roughly 200 ms observation must not be compared directly with 135.788 ms as a
code-only speedup: today's unchanged C3 already measures 147.401 ms.

Separate staged probes measure C3/candidate tracing and lowering at
**45.900/36.448 ms**, and executable compilation at **86.266/80.821 ms**. These
altered-path measurements use separate fresh processes; they are not summed or
substituted for the whole public-call observation.
