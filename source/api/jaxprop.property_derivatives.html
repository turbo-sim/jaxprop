jaxprop.property_derivatives module#

Property partial derivatives with separate primal-state provenance and units.

class jaxprop.property_derivatives.PropertyDerivatives(primal, derivatives, input_names, *, method='autodiff')[source]#

Bases: Module

Local two-input partials, distinct from ordinary thermodynamic values.

shape is the primal broadcast shape; every derivative array has derivative_shape == shape + (2,). The final axis follows input_names. primal retains the physical state, status, validity and clipping flags.

jaxprop.property_derivatives.get_props_gradient(fluid, input_pair, x, y, method='auto', eps_rel=1e-06, eps_abs=1e-06, *, errors='mask')[source]#

Return local two-coordinate property partials and their primal state.

Native AD linearizes one numerical property dictionary and applies its JVP to two unit directions. Array/broadcast inputs produce shape + (2,) local partials, never a full cross-element Jacobian. The FD alternative uses independent centered perturbations. Differentiation follows the selected mask/clip policy, including zero sensitivities outside clip bounds.
